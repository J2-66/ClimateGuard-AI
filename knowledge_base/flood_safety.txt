Flood risk depends on several interacting factors, including rainfall intensity and duration, river or drainage conditions, land elevation, soil saturation, land cover, drainage capacity and forecast weather.

A prototype flood assistant should be treated as decision support rather than an official warning system. Users should rely on local disaster-management authorities and official weather/flood alerts for emergency decisions.

During flooding, avoid driving or walking through moving or unknown-depth water, move to safer elevated areas when instructed, protect essential documents and follow evacuation orders from authorities.

Flood Safety Guide

Flooding can happen because of heavy rainfall, river overflow, storm surge, drainage problems, or other environmental and infrastructure conditions.

Before a flood, people should monitor information from local authorities and emergency management agencies. Important documents, medicines, drinking water, food, flashlights, and emergency supplies should be kept ready.

During flooding, people should avoid walking or driving through moving floodwater. Water depth and current strength can be difficult to judge, and floodwater may contain hazards.

People should move to safer locations when instructed by local authorities. Electrical equipment should not be handled when standing in water.

After flooding, people should follow official instructions before returning to affected areas. Floodwater can create risks from contaminated water, damaged structures, electrical hazards, and debris.

ClimateGuard AI provides educational information and should not replace official emergency warnings or instructions from local authorities.

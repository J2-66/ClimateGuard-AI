Carbon Footprint Guide

A carbon footprint represents the greenhouse gas emissions associated with an activity, person, organization, product, or service.

Common sources of household emissions include electricity use, transportation, heating or cooking fuels, air travel, food, and consumption of goods.

Reducing electricity consumption through efficient appliances and responsible energy use can reduce associated emissions.

Transportation emissions can sometimes be reduced by walking, cycling, public transportation, carpooling, or using more efficient vehicles where practical.

Reducing unnecessary consumption, reusing products, recycling appropriate materials, and reducing food waste can also contribute to lower environmental impacts.

Carbon footprint calculations are estimates. Results depend on the emission factors, geographic region, energy mix, travel type, and assumptions used by the calculator.

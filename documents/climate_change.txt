Climate Change Overview

Climate change refers to long-term changes in temperature and weather patterns. Human activities, especially the burning of fossil fuels such as coal, oil, and natural gas, have increased the concentration of greenhouse gases in the atmosphere.

Carbon dioxide, methane, and nitrous oxide are important greenhouse gases. These gases trap some heat in the atmosphere and contribute to global warming.

Climate change can affect ecosystems, agriculture, water resources, human health, and communities. Its effects can vary by region.

Reducing greenhouse gas emissions can help limit future warming. Common approaches include improving energy efficiency, increasing the use of low-carbon energy sources, reducing unnecessary fuel consumption, protecting forests, and improving resource efficiency.

Individuals can contribute by using energy efficiently, choosing lower-emission transportation when practical, reducing waste, and making informed consumption choices.

Climate Action Guide

Climate action includes activities that reduce greenhouse gas emissions and actions that help communities adapt to climate-related risks.

Mitigation focuses on reducing the causes of climate change. Examples include renewable energy, energy efficiency, cleaner transportation, reducing waste, and protecting ecosystems.

Adaptation focuses on reducing vulnerability to climate impacts. Examples include flood preparedness, improved drainage, early warning systems, heat preparedness, resilient infrastructure, and community planning.

Individuals can take practical actions such as reducing unnecessary energy use, using lower-emission transportation when possible, reducing waste, conserving water, and learning about local environmental risks.

Communities and governments can support climate resilience through planning, infrastructure improvements, environmental protection, emergency preparedness, and public awareness.
